# ADR-003: Hermes through ACP

- Status: accepted for v0.2
- Date: 2026-08-25

## Context

JACK-K must remain accountable, recoverable, least-privileged, and safe under hostile or mistaken agent output.

## Decision

Integrate Hermes through structured ACP JSON-RPC in a dedicated service, initially restricted to L0/L1 planning and research. JACK-K validates every plan and permission request.

## Consequences

This boundary adds operational work and may reduce convenience, but it makes authority explicit, testable, attributable to a ticket, and recoverable. Any expansion requires a versioned policy, automated tests, and human approval.
